Remix Forge Publisher

Remix Forge Publisher is a personal desktop tool that publishes short-form music remix videos created by its developer to the developer's own TikTok account, using TikTok's official Content Posting API.

How it works

  1. Videos are rendered locally with an FFmpeg pipeline (chorus cut → crossfade loop → audio preset → 9:16 video with a background image).
  2. The owner reviews each rendered video and writes its caption and privacy level into a local queue file.
  3. The tool authenticates the owner's TikTok account via Login Kit (OAuth) and posts the next queued video via the Content Posting API — at most a few posts per day.

Scope of the app

Single-user by design: the App is not distributed, offers no service to third parties, collects no data from anyone other than the app owner, and never reads, comments on, or interacts with any other user's content.
